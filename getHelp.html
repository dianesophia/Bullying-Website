Support

You Don’t Have to Handle It Alone.

Asking for help is not weakness. If bullying is happening to you or someone you know, reaching out to a trusted person can be an important first step.

Who Can You Talk To?

At School

  • Teacher
  • Class adviser
  • Guidance counselor
  • School nurse
  • School administrator
  • Trusted school staff member

At Home

  • Parent
  • Guardian
  • Older sibling
  • Relative
  • Another trusted adult

When Asking for Help

You can start with something as simple as:

“Something has been happening at school, and I don’t know how to handle it. Can I talk to you?”

You do not need to have the perfect words or explain everything immediately.

If You’re Reporting Bullying

Try to share:

  • What happened
  • Who was involved
  • When and where it happened
  • How often it has happened
  • Whether anyone witnessed it
  • Any evidence you have

If You Feel Unsafe Right Now

Move toward a safe place and seek help from a trusted adult or appropriate emergency service. Your immediate safety comes first.
