Get Help

If you are being bullied, or you see someone who needs support, you do not have to handle it alone.

Talk to a teacher, school counselor, parent, or another trusted adult. You can also email us at help@standtogether.org.

If you are in immediate danger, tell an adult nearby or contact local emergency services.
