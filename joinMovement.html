What Can You Do Today?

At School

  • Include someone who is being left out.
  • Report bullying.
  • Start a kindness campaign.
  • Encourage respectful conversations.

Online

  • Don’t share humiliating content.
  • Report harmful posts.
  • Don’t participate in comment attacks.
  • Use your platform to spread positivity.

In Your Community

  • Talk about bullying.
  • Support anti-bullying activities.
  • Encourage others to speak up.
  • Be someone others feel safe approaching.

The Movement Starts With One Choice.

Stand up for someone. Speak out against bullying. Choose kindness. Create change.
