A safer community starts with all of us

No one deserves to feel afraid, unwanted, or alone at school. Bullying can happen to anyone, and it can leave an impact that lasts far beyond the moment.

Whether you experience it, witness it, or have taken part in it, you have the power to make a different choice. This website is here to help you understand bullying, know what to do, find support, and become part of a kinder community.

Remember

Speaking up can be difficult. Staying silent can feel easier. But one voice can be the beginning of change.
